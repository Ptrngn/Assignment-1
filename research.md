My five year goals deeply connect with what I am learning at university, especially my goal of building a career in UX and design. Through projects, feedback, research, and problem-solving, I am developing skills that I can use after graduation. Oudkerk Pool et al. (2020) explain that portfolios can show a student's development through experiences, performance and feedback. This connects to my goal of building a strong design portfolio that shows my growth. However, goals like financial independence, travel, and lifestyle are more personal and are not measured through academic learning outcomes. Overall, university is helping me build the skills I need for my career and future.
